Technology has transformed the way students access information, collaborate with peers, and demonstrate understanding. Digital tools enable personalized pacing, immediate feedback, and richer forms of assessment than traditional worksheets allow. However, it is important to emphasize that technology amplifies rather than replaces good pedagogy; without clear learning goals, even the most sophisticated platform can become a distraction. Teachers therefore remain central to the process, guiding interpretation and helping students evaluate the reliability of what they encounter. In summary, the most effective implementations treat technology as a means to deepen engagement and provide timely support, not as an end in itself.
