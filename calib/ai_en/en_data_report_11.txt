The analysis covers three reporting periods and evaluates performance across four operational dimensions. Overall, throughput increased modestly while error rates declined, indicating that the process adjustments introduced in the second period had a measurable effect. It should be noted, however, that the sample size for the third period is substantially smaller, which limits the strength of any conclusions drawn from it. Furthermore, the observed improvement is concentrated in two of the four dimensions, suggesting that the underlying causes are structural rather than systemic. Accordingly, we recommend extending the measurement window before committing to further changes, and we advise against treating the current figures as evidence of a durable trend.
