Developing resilience is less about avoiding difficulty and more about building the capacity to respond effectively when challenges arise. One useful starting point is to separate the facts of a situation from the interpretation you place on them, because this distinction often reveals options that were previously obscured. Additionally, cultivating supportive relationships provides both perspective and practical assistance during demanding periods. It is also important to note that rest is not a reward for productivity but a prerequisite for sustained performance. By approaching setbacks as information rather than verdicts, you create the conditions for continued growth. Ultimately, resilience emerges from repeated, small acts of recovery rather than a single decisive moment.
