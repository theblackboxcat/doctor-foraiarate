The question of whether artificial intelligence benefits society has generated considerable debate in recent years. On the one hand, AI technologies have significantly improved efficiency in fields such as medicine, transportation, and education. For example, diagnostic systems can analyze medical images faster than human specialists, which may lead to earlier treatment and better outcomes. On the other hand, concerns about privacy, employment, and accountability cannot be ignored. In conclusion, the impact of artificial intelligence ultimately depends on how it is designed, regulated, and used. Rather than viewing it as inherently good or harmful, we should adopt a balanced perspective that encourages innovation while establishing appropriate safeguards for the public interest.
