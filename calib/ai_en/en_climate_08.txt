Climate change refers to long-term shifts in temperature and weather patterns, driven primarily by human activities such as the combustion of fossil fuels. These activities increase the concentration of greenhouse gases in the atmosphere, which in turn intensifies the natural greenhouse effect. As a result, average global temperatures have risen, contributing to more frequent heatwaves, shifting precipitation patterns, and rising sea levels. It is important to recognize that the impacts are not distributed evenly; vulnerable communities often experience the most severe consequences despite contributing least to the problem. Addressing this challenge requires coordinated action across mitigation, adaptation, and financing, supported by transparent measurement and sustained international cooperation.
