Artificial intelligence refers to the ability of computer systems to perform tasks that typically require human intelligence, such as understanding language, recognizing patterns, and making decisions. These systems are trained on large volumes of data, allowing them to identify relationships that would be difficult for people to detect manually. It is important to note that modern AI models are not conscious and do not "understand" information in the way humans do; rather, they estimate which output is most likely given the input they receive. Consequently, their performance depends heavily on the quality and representativeness of the training data. Furthermore, AI systems can inherit biases present in that data, which is why careful evaluation, transparent documentation, and ongoing monitoring are essential components of responsible deployment.
